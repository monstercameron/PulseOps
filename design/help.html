Connecting Data

We support direct integrations with QuickBooks Online, Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Square, and Clover. We also accept file imports (Excel, Google Sheets, CSV) and bank statement exports. If you use a tool that's not listed, contact us — we add new connectors regularly based on customer demand.

Yes. All connections use OAuth (read-only where available), data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never store raw credentials. We access only the data needed to generate your brief and do not share your business data with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

On initial setup, we pull up to 24 months of historical data from connected sources (where available). This gives us enough context to identify trends and seasonal patterns in your job types. The longer you use PulseOps, the more accurate the benchmarking becomes.

Your Brief

Your brief is delivered by email every Monday at 7:00 AM in your local time zone. You can also view it in the PulseOps web portal at any time. Full Operations Pack subscribers also receive a daily snapshot every weekday morning.

Every item in your brief shows the underlying data behind it — which jobs, which dates, and how confident we are. If something looks off, you can flag it directly from the brief. Flagged items are reviewed, and your correction teaches PulseOps more about how your business is structured. Over time this reduces incorrect recommendations significantly.

Yes. You can add additional email recipients to your brief delivery so your bookkeeper, office manager, or business partner receives a copy automatically. Each brief has a shareable link that doesn't require login. You can manage recipients in your account settings.
