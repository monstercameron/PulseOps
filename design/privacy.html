Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

PulseOps, Inc. ("PulseOps," "we," "us," or "our") operates the PulseOps platform and website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our services. By using PulseOps, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at privacy@pulseops.io.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information. When you create a PulseOps account, we collect your name, email address, business name, and billing information. We do not store full payment card numbers — payment processing is handled by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS compliant.

Business Data. To generate your weekly brief, PulseOps connects to your business software and financial accounts. This includes job records, invoice data, payroll summaries, bank transaction data, and scheduling information. We access only the data necessary to produce your brief. This data is used solely to provide the PulseOps service and is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Usage Data. We collect information about how you interact with PulseOps — pages visited, features used, email opens and clicks, and browser/device information. This helps us improve the product.

Communications. If you contact our support team, we retain those communications to provide support and improve our responses.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and deliver your Cash & Margin Brief and any other reports or alerts you've configured
  • To improve the accuracy of our data processing and recommendations over time
  • To communicate with you about your account, new features, and important service updates
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Connections & Third-Party Access

When you connect a third-party tool (such as QuickBooks, Jobber, or ServiceTitan), we use OAuth authorization where supported, which means we never see your password for those services. We request read-only access wherever available and never modify data in your connected systems.

We do not share your business data with any third party except as follows:

  • Service providers who help us operate the platform (cloud hosting, email delivery, payment processing). These providers are bound by data processing agreements and may not use your data for their own purposes.
  • Legal requirements — if required to comply with a court order, subpoena, or applicable law.
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger or acquisition, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you in advance.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data, including:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Role-based access controls limiting which employees can access customer data
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • SOC 2 Type II compliance (report available upon request under NDA)

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your business data for as long as your account is active and for up to 90 days after you cancel. After that period, we delete or anonymize your data. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting privacy@pulseops.io.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@pulseops.io. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your login session, remember preferences, and understand how users interact with our website. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, but some features of PulseOps may not function correctly without them. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

PulseOps is a business-to-business service and is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of PulseOps after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at:
